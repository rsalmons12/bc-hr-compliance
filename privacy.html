Who we are

BC HR & Compliance operates this secure portal for its client companies and their authorised staff.

What we collect

Your name, email address and company; documents uploaded to the portal; policy acknowledgements; compliance task updates; and any complaint or incident reports you submit.

How we use it

Only to provide HR and compliance services to your company. We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see it

Each company can only see its own information. Complaint and incident reports are visible only to the person who submitted them and to BC HR & Compliance staff.

Where it is stored

Portal data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, using encrypted connections and access controls.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting BC HR & Compliance.
