Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses, typically 5 to 250 employees, that don't yet have a dedicated HR department or need extra support.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. We provide HR and compliance consulting. Where a matter needs legal advice, we'll recommend you speak with an employment lawyer.

How is pricing structured?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price after the assessment. Retainers are a flat monthly fee based on company size.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. Most work is done remotely, with on-site visits available for training and investigations.
